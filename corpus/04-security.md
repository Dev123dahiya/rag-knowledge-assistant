# Information security

## Passwords and sign-in
All accounts must use the company password manager and multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Never share credentials over chat or email.

## Devices
Laptops must have full-disk encryption switched on and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within 1 hour.

## Phishing
Forward suspicious emails to security@nimbus.example. Do not click links or open attachments in them. Simulated phishing tests run every quarter.
