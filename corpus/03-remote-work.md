# Remote and hybrid work

## Hybrid schedule
Teams work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The other three days are remote by default. Managers may approve a fully remote arrangement for up to 3 months at a time.

## Home office equipment
New joiners receive a one-time allowance of 15,000 rupees for a desk, chair or monitor. Laptops are provided by IT and remain company property.

## Working from another country
Working from outside India for more than 14 days in a year needs written approval from HR and Legal because of tax rules.
