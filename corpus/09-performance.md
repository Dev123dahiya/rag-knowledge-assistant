# Performance reviews

## Cycle
Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from three peers and a manager assessment.

## Ratings
There are four ratings: exceeds expectations, meets expectations, partially meets, and does not meet. Ratings are calibrated across teams before they are shared.

## Promotions
Promotion cases are considered in the April cycle only. A case needs a written document from the manager and evidence of working at the next level for at least 6 months.
