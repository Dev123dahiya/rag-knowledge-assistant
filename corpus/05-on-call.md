# On-call and incidents

## Rotation
Each engineering team runs a weekly on-call rotation that hands over on Monday at 10:00. The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes.

## Severity levels
SEV1 means customers cannot use the product; it requires an incident commander and updates every 30 minutes. SEV2 is a major feature degraded. SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive 2,500 rupees per weekday and 4,000 rupees per weekend day on rotation. A blameless post-incident review is due within 5 working days of a SEV1.
