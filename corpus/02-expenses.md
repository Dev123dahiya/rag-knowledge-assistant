# Expense reimbursement

## Submitting a claim
Submit claims in the Finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemised receipt for anything above 500 rupees. Claims are paid with the next payroll run if approved before the 20th of the month.

## Travel
Book flights at least 14 days ahead where possible. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Hotel stays are capped at 7,000 rupees per night in metro cities and 4,500 rupees elsewhere.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance while travelling is 1,200 rupees. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
