# Onboarding

## First week
New joiners get their laptop and accounts on day one, meet their onboarding buddy, and complete security training by the end of the first week.

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. During probation either side can end employment with 15 days of notice. After probation the notice period is 60 days.

## First project
Engineers ship a small starter change to production within their first two weeks, paired with their buddy.
