# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at two days per month. Up to 8 unused days carry over into the next year; anything beyond that lapses on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.
