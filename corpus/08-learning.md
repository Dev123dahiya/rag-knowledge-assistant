# Learning and development

## Budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 40,000 rupees for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Certifications
The company pays exam fees for approved cloud and security certifications once per certification. A second attempt is at the employee's own cost.

## Study time
Employees may use up to 4 hours per week of working time for learning, agreed with their manager.
